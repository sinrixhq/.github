Sinrix is where you create your brand. Every brand comes with a co-founder you name and nine agent teams, 34 agents in all, that write, research, build and support, on a private server of its own at its own address.

## How it works

1. **Name your co-founder.** Pick a name for them. Tell them what the company does, or point them at your website and they read it.
2. **Connect your apps.** Gmail, Slack, Notion, Shopify and hundreds more, or any MCP server. Each person connects their own accounts.
3. **Ask for the work.** Ask the way you would ask a person. Your co-founder hands it to the right team and brings back what they made.
